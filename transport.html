Destination Highlights

  • Culture & Heritage: Udupi is known for its ancient temples, rich traditions, and famous coastal cuisine.
  • Top Attractions: Sri Krishna Temple, Malpe Beach, St. Mary's Island, and Maravanthe Beach.
  • Festivals: Krishna Janmashtami and Paryaya draw visitors with vibrant rituals and celebrations.
  • Best Season: November–February offers perfect weather for sightseeing and beach visits.
  • Activities: Try scuba diving, coastal hiking, or explore local art, markets, and beachside cafes.
  • Photography Tips: Capture sunrises at Malpe, respect temple photo rules, and share with #UdupiVibes.
